## Instrument design (fixed)

Characters move from single event types to **predicates**: `(event_type, agent_role, syntactic_frame)`
triples, where:
- `event_type ∈` frozen codebook v2 (30 canonical codes, homoglyph/typo-validated)
- `agent_role ∈` {creator, trickster, deity, hero, sibling, parent, animal, monster, human-ordinary, cosmic-entity, unspecified} — 11 slots (Propp-derived dramatis personae reduced to comparably-required minimum)
- `syntactic_frame ∈` {transitive, reflexive, reciprocal, stative, locative-motion, speech-act, possessive-chain} (7 frames)

Predicate space ≈ 30×11×7 ≈ 2,310 theoretical cells; empirically occupied cells expected in the
hundreds — an order of magnitude above the 30-code floor. Chain lengths are normalized to
**episode units** (maximal runs with one primary agent; runs of identical consecutive codes collapse;
target length band 8–40 predicates; chains outside the band get flagged, not dropped).
