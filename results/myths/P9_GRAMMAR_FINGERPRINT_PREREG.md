# P9: Grammar-as-Horizon-Fingerprint (registered 2026-10-17, Jan-confirmed scope)

## Claim under test (within-horizon only)
Event-chain grammar patterns identify which tradition within a cultural horizon a myth
derives from. NOT a between-horizon phylogenetic claim (that remains X-tier per κ round 2).

## Pilot evidence (ancient catalog, n=22, single pipeline)
- Labeled nearest-centroid, leave-one-out: grammar event-type profiles macro acc 0.58
  vs majority-class 0.36; permutation p=0.0045 (2000 shuffles). Mytheme profiles 0.45, p=0.03.
- Grammar BEATS content at tradition recovery within the Near Eastern macro-horizon.
- Confusion structure: Ugaritic 1.00, Mesopotamian 0.86, Hittite absorbed toward Ugaritic
  (5/8) — consistent with shared NW-Semitic/Anatolian literary register.

## Decisive follow-up (preregistered prediction)
Blind deployment: hold out a tradition never coded by the current pipeline, classify by
grammar centroids, check provenance. PREDICTION (ex-ante): held-out placement above the
permuted null if the fingerprint is horizon-real; chance-level if it is pipeline artifact.
Rival prediction registered: if grammar accuracy collapses on independent coding, the
0.58 is pipeline fingerprint, not horizon fingerprint — resolving the single-coder confound
without a third kappa round.

## Tier
M (provisional). Cannot rise to S until the blind deployment passes on independently-coded
material. Confounded by: single pipeline, 4 unbalanced classes, shared contact history.
